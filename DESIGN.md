# Sanctuary Elegance Design System

## Brand & Aesthetic Vision
This design system embodies serene Islamic hospitality, blending sacred reverence with modern high-end travel concierges. Designed for pilgrims seeking an elevated, seamless journey for Umrah and Hajj, the aesthetic is deeply grounded in tranquility, trust, and luxury.

The design combines **Modern Minimalist Hospitality** with subtle **Glassmorphism and Sacred Geometry**. Visual weight is airy and unhurried, reflecting spiritual stillness and meticulous organization. Interfaces honor right-to-left (RTL) reading flow naturally, offering balanced margins, soft tactile layered surfaces, luminous brass/gold accents, and quiet emerald anchoring.

## Colors
- **Primary (`#003527` / `#064E3B`)**: Deep Emerald Islamic Green. Represents spiritual tranquility, life, and sacred heritage. Used for dominant brand moments, primary CTAs, active states, and grounding structural containers.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` / `#904D00` / `#FE932C`)**: Radiant Sand Gold. Signifies divine warmth, luxury, hospitality, badge highlights, and star ratings.
- **Tertiary (`#262E42` / `#0F172A`)**: Deep Navy Slate. Introduces contemporary architectural contrast, premium editorial typography, and high-legibility dark containers.
- **Surface & Background (`#FBF9F5`)**: Warm Pearl Alabaster canvas, eliminating clinical coldness.

## Typography
- **Spiritual & Headlines:** `Amiri`, `Amiri Quran`, `Noto Serif` for classical reverence.
- **Functional UI & Labels:** `Cairo`, `Noto Sans` for rapid legibility and crisp numbers.
